## Voice: "Your Sharp Friend"

Direct, confident, conversational. A friend who happens to know skincare and doesn't make
it weird.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| "One step. Done." | "Explore our carefully curated range" |
| "The product works." | "We believe this could help" |
| "50 early testers. Zero refund requests." | "Clinically proven by dermatologists" |
| "That greasy feeling? Gone." | "Eliminate excess sebum production" |
| "Look sharper" | "Anti-aging miracle for him" |
| "Everything your skin needs. Nothing it doesn't." | "Advanced 6-step regimen" |

## Sentence rules

- Short, punchy, declarative.
- Lead with benefits, not features.
- Real numbers: "15 seconds", "50 testers", "$38".
- State facts. Never "we think" / "we believe".
- Headlines are ALWAYS uppercase.
- Colorado/altitude is the credibility anchor — use it.

## Don't Use — hard bans

**Words:** curated · elevated · artisanal · lifestyle (as buzzword) · we think · we believe ·
clinically proven (without a real trial) · miracle · anti-aging miracle · advanced regimen ·
explore (as a CTA verb) · carefully · luxurious · luxury · indulge · pampering · any
fragrance or scent language (the product is fragrance-free).

**Punctuation and formatting:** no emojis anywhere · no exclamation marks in headlines ·
no question marks in headlines (rhetorical questions are fine in body copy) · no long-form
marketing copy inside a heading.

**Other:** never name a competitor.

These bans apply to on-image text as much as to primary text. Check every headline and
overlay against this list before it goes into an image prompt.
