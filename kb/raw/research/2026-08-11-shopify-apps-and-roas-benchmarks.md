## WebSearch Supplemental Results

- **Ecom App Guide / bestecommerceapps.com** — Reddit roundup of most-recommended Shopify apps: Klaviyo (cited as adding 10-30% incremental revenue once flows are tuned), Judge.me (5-15% conversion lift when reviews render on the PDP), Recharge for subscriptions. Community bias is toward ROI and clean Shopify integration over feature lists.
- **PainPointMap** — r/shopify and r/ecommerce are the two primary seller subreddits; r/dropship and r/FulfillmentByAmazon are adjacent for sourcing/logistics.
- **easyappsecom.com (Beauty & Cosmetics app guide)** — Beauty buyers read reviews more than any other category; filterable reviews (skin type, age, concern) outperform an unsegmented review block. Recommends consolidating single-purpose apps.
- **Loopwork** — Argues beauty subscriptions need flexible bundling (mix variants in one subscription) because of multi-product routines; positions Loop against Recharge.
- **MultiVariants / Giftkart guides** — Bundles + loyalty called out as the two AOV levers specific to beauty.
- **hawky.ai ROAS Benchmarks 2026** — Beauty & personal care industry benchmark ~4.2x. Google Ads median ~3.5-3.7x, Meta ~1.86-2.2x, TikTok ~1.4x.
- **Prooflytics / dtcroas.com** — Google Shopping 4-8x, Meta 2-4x, TikTok 2-4x on direct attribution. Notes attribution windows differ (Google 30-day click, Meta 7-day click + 1-day view), so cross-channel ROAS is not apples-to-apples.
- **adamigo.ai (Meta ROAS benchmarks)** — B2C ecommerce Meta ROAS is seasonal: 4-5x in Q4, 2-2.5x in Jan/Feb, 3-3.5x through summer.
- **usedaymark.io / shopify-fee-calc.com** — For a DTC skincare brand at 65% gross margin and $55 AOV, breakeven ROAS is ~1.5x. At 35% COGS with Shopify Payments, breakeven lands ~2.0-2.5x; "healthy" is 3-4x.
- **Eightx (MER guide) + Shopify MER blog** — Breakeven MER = 1 / contribution margin. 30% CM → breakeven MER 3.3; 40% CM → 2.5. Healthy blended MER target 3.0-5.0.
- **Triple Whale / convertcart / easyappsecom ROAS guides** — Three fastest ROAS levers: raise AOV (bundles/upsells), cut CPA via creative + landing page, reallocate budget from low- to high-ROAS audiences. A 10% AOV lift is a 10% ROAS lift. Homepage traffic kills ROAS — send paid to the PDP or a dedicated LP. Suggested split: 60% prospecting, 25% retargeting, 15% creative testing. Test creative at $50-100/variant before scaling.
- **TikTok as demand-gen** — Several benchmark write-ups note pausing TikTok spend drops Google branded search volume and shrinks Meta retargeting pools, i.e. its measured ROAS understates its contribution.
