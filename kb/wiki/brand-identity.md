## Brand Archetype & Positioning

**Archetype:** "Your Sharp Friend"
**Positioning:** Direct, confident, conversational. Like a friend who knows skincare but does not make it weird.
**Frame:** Performance gear, not a beauty brand. Skincare as equipment, not ritual.
**Origin:** Breckenridge, Colorado. Altitude and harsh elements are the credibility anchor.
**Target:** Active men 25-40 who are skeptical of skincare, short on time, and hard on their skin. They buy gear, not grooming products.

**Brand Pillars:**

| Pillar | Expression |
|---|---|
| **Simplicity** | One product. One step. No routines. |
| **Performance** | Clinical-grade ingredients at proven concentrations. Results in days, not months. |
| **Credibility** | Formulated in Colorado. Real numbers. Real testers. No jargon. |
| **Urgency** | Founding batch. Limited run. $38 founding price increases at launch. |

## Voice Rules

### We Are vs We Are NOT

| Attribute | We Are | We Are NOT |
|---|---|---|
| Direct | "One step. Done." | "Explore our carefully curated range" |
| Confident | "The product works." | "We believe this could help" |
| Honest | "50 early testers. Zero refund requests." | "Clinically proven by dermatologists" |
| Conversational | "That greasy feeling? Gone." | "Eliminate excess sebum production" |
| Masculine-neutral | "Look sharper" | "Anti-aging miracle for him" |
| Anti-complexity | "Everything your skin needs. Nothing it doesn't." | "Advanced 6-step regimen" |

### Sentence Style

- Short, punchy, declarative
- Period-terminated or no punctuation
- Lead with benefits, not features
- Use real numbers: "15 seconds", "50 testers", "$38"
- State facts. Never "we think" or "we believe"
- Headings ALWAYS uppercase
- Second person ("your skin", not "one's skin")
- Active voice only

### Tone by Channel

| Channel | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Website | Authoritative, spec-driven | "Niacinamide at 5% reduces how much oil your skin produces -- not just blotting it." |
| Social | Slightly more casual, punchy | "PEAK PROTECTION. DONE." |
| Email | Direct, personal | "Your bottle ships Monday. Here's what to expect in week one." |
| Blog | Educational, problem-first | "Your face loses 25% more moisture above 8,000 feet. Here's the fix." |
| Packaging | Minimal, clinical | "Performance Daily Face Cream. 1.7 fl oz (50 ml)." |
| Support | Warm but concise | "Got it. Replacement ships today. You'll have tracking by tonight." |

### Approved Vocabulary

| Category | Terms |
|---|---|
| Product | Gear, cream, formula, layer, barrier, armor |
| Performance | Specs, engineered, clinical-grade, altitude-rated, precision |
| Actions | Repair, absorb, defend, protect, control, rebuild, calm |
| Results | Matte, invisible, zero residue, instant, measurable |
| Time | 15 seconds, day one, week one, 4-8 weeks |

## Banned Words

**Never use in any brand copy:**
- curated
- elevated
- artisanal
- lifestyle (as buzzword)
- we think / we believe
- clinically proven (unless backed by actual clinical trial)
- miracle / anti-aging miracle
- advanced regimen
- explore (as CTA verb)
- carefully
- luxurious / luxury
- indulge / pampering
- any fragrance/scent language (product is fragrance-free)
- revolutionary / game-changing / incredible / amazing / best-ever
- routine / regimen / multi-step / system / collection
- manly / rugged / alpha / bro / dude
- helps improve / may reduce / supports healthy-looking

## Banned Formatting

- No emojis on any brand touchpoint
- No exclamation marks in headlines
- No question marks in headlines (rhetorical questions OK in body copy)
- No long-form marketing copy in headings
- No decorative or script fonts

**Formula 1 — Benefit stack:**
```

PUT IT ON. FORGET ABOUT IT.
EVERYTHING YOUR SKIN NEEDS. NOTHING IT DOESN'T.

```

```

DONE.

## Visual Identity

### Design System Note

Two design systems exist in the brand assets. The production site (`baselayer-lovable-export`) uses the **monochrome dark system** from `_brand-context.md` and `identity/identity.md`. The formal brand guidelines (`BaseLayer_Brand_Guidelines.md`) define an **Alpine Navy + Ascent Orange system**. The live HeroSection uses Alpine Navy (`#1A2F4C`) as the background with Ascent Orange (`#F95D1A`) for the CTA and input focus ring, indicating the production site is a hybrid. When in doubt, match what the live code does.

| `--secondary` | `#1A1A1A` | Section alternation |

| `--surface-light-muted` | `#666666` | Muted text on light surfaces |

| Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Amber | `#FBBF24` | Star ratings, highlight badges only |

### Color System (Brand Guidelines -- Alpine Navy + Ascent Orange)

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Alpine Navy | `#1A2F4C` | Backgrounds, headlines, primary text, logo |
| Accent | Ascent Orange | `#F95D1A` | Logo period, CTAs, interactive states |
| Neutral | Glacier Grey | `#ABB3BB` | Borders, secondary text, dividers |
| Background | White | `#FFFFFF` | Page backgrounds, cards, labels |

| Dark extended | Stone | `#2C3E50` | Secondary dark surfaces |

**Color hierarchy rule:** 70% Alpine Navy + White, 20% Glacier Grey, 10% Ascent Orange. Orange is surgical, not decorative -- max 2 placements per screen.

### Color Rules (Universal)

- Brand is monochrome at its core. Color is functional, not decorative.
- High contrast always. No mid-gray-on-gray.
- Hero overlays: `gradient-to-right from-black/70 via-black/50 to-black/25` (desktop), `gradient-to-top from-black/80 via-black/50 to-black/30` (mobile).

### Typography (Production Site)

|---|---|---|---|
| Headings | DM Sans | 700, 800, 900 | Uppercase, tight leading (0.9), tracking-tight |
| Body | Inter | 400, 500, 600 | Sentence case, relaxed leading |

```

### Typography (Brand Guidelines)

**Typeface:** Montserrat (Google Fonts) -- sole typeface for all communications.

| Element | Weight | Size (Web) | Case | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hero headline | Bold (700) | 48-72px | UPPERCASE | +0.05em |
| Section headline | Bold (700) | 32-40px | UPPERCASE | +0.04em |
| Subheadline | SemiBold (600) | 20-24px | Title Case or UPPERCASE | +0.02em |
| Body | Regular (400) | 16-18px | Sentence case | Normal |
| Caption/Label | Medium (500) | 12-14px | UPPERCASE | +0.06em |
| Button/CTA | Bold (700) | 14-16px | UPPERCASE | +0.08em |

### Typography Rules (Universal)

- Headings ALWAYS uppercase. No exceptions.
- No decorative or script fonts. Geometric sans-serif only.
- Tight leading on headlines (0.9-1.2).
- Wide tracking on small labels (0.2em-0.35em).
- Font weight carries hierarchy, not color.
- Max body width: 680px (~65 characters per line).
- Line height: 1.1-1.2 for headlines, 1.5-1.6 for body.

### Layout

- **Border radius:** `0rem` -- sharp corners everywhere. The brand is angular.
- **Hero overlays:** Gradient overlays for text readability on photography.
- **Image format:** WebP with responsive srcSet (480w, 768w, 1200w, 1920w).
- **Lazy loading:** Below-fold images only. Hero is `fetchPriority="high"`, `loading="eager"`.

## Logo Usage

### Primary Wordmark

**Format:** `BASE LAYER.` -- two words, always uppercase, always with terminal period.
**Signature element:** Round orange period (Ascent Orange `#F95D1A`). Non-negotiable -- always present in full-color applications. Circle, not square -- it is Montserrat 800's own period. Corrected 2026-08-12: this entry previously said "square, not circle" and named the orange `#F35D1A`. The square period described a broken PNG export (a fallback system font renders a square period), not the real mark; `#F35D1A` is retired in favor of the single brand orange `#F95D1A`.

Superseded 2026-08-12: an earlier thin-weight wordmark with horizontal rules above and below was in use across `brand/assets/logo/` and `public/lovable-uploads/`. It was not the real mark (wrong weight, no period) and has been deleted. Every logo file now derives from the outlined Montserrat 800 pack.

### Logo Hierarchy

1. Full wordmark -- use whenever space allows (min 120px digital, 30mm print)
2. Padded/contained wordmark -- on complex or photographic backgrounds
3. BL. icon -- only when full wordmark cannot fit (min 32px digital, 8mm print)
4. Single-color -- only when color reproduction is impossible

### Logo Clear Space

Minimum clear zone: cap height of the "B" on all four sides.

### Logo Misuse (Never Do)

- Rotate, skew, or distort
- Change letter or word spacing
- Replace the round period with a square or other shape
- Use lowercase or mixed case
- Place on busy backgrounds without container variant
- Add shadows, outlines, gradients, or effects
- Recolor orange period (except in single-color variants)
- Stack "BASE" over "LAYER" (reserved for hero/editorial only)
- Separate horizontal rules from the mark (production site variant)

## Photography

**Primary style:** Dramatic, high-contrast, editorial. Documentary-grade, not studio beauty.

### Product Shots

- Clean, dark surfaces (stone, slate, concrete, granite, packed snow)
- Single product focus, no clutter
- Dramatic shadows, minimal props
- Studio lighting with warm accent, or natural alpine light
- Product label must be legible in hero shots
- Never plain white/grey backgrounds (except marketplace listings)

### Lifestyle Shots

- Active outdoor contexts: skiing, hiking, morning routine, job site
- Natural light preferred: golden hour, overcast mountain light, high-altitude midday
- Candid > posed. Expression: neutral to quietly confident, never exaggerated.
- Men 25-40, diverse ethnicities, athletic builds
- Colorado outdoor settings: mountains, altitude, real light
- Wardrobe: technical outerwear, muted colors (navy, grey, olive, charcoal). No neon, no other brand logos.
- Product occupies max 30-40% of frame in lifestyle shots

### Color Grading

- Cool-to-neutral, desaturated mid-tones
- Blues and greys dominate environment
- Skin tones remain natural and warm -- never orange-shifted
- High contrast: deep shadows, bright highlights

### Composition

- Asymmetric compositions with strong leading lines (ridgelines, trails, edges)
- Generous negative space for text overlays
- Environment dominates -- landscape is the co-star

### Non-Negotiable Rules

- No stock photo aesthetic. Ever.
- No soft/rounded aesthetics
- Colorado authentic -- real environments, real altitude, real light
- Real men, real skin -- visible pores, natural texture, no airbrushing
- No models in studio-white backgrounds
- No emojis on any image asset
- Masculine-neutral -- confident, not aggressive

### Image Treatment (Web)

- WebP format with responsive srcSet: 480w, 768w, 1200w, 1920w
- Lazy loading for below-fold images
- Hero gradient overlays for text readability
- `fetchPriority="high"` and `loading="eager"` for hero images

## The Period Rule

Terminal periods in headlines and taglines are a deliberate brand choice. They echo the logo's signature orange period and reinforce the definitive, no-nonsense voice.

Every statement is a conclusion, not a question or suggestion.

```
ONE CREAM. 15 SECONDS. DONE.
PEAK PROTECTION. DONE.
EVERYTHING YOUR SKIN NEEDS. NOTHING IT DOESN'T.
```

## Brand Consistency Checklist

Before delivering any brand output, verify:

1. **Voice:** No banned words? Headings uppercase? No emojis? No exclamation marks in headlines?
2. **Visual:** Correct palette? Sharp corners (0rem radius)? Correct typefaces? Dark mode default?
3. **Photography:** Real skin texture visible? No stock aesthetic? Dramatic lighting? No white backgrounds?
4. **Product accuracy:** Correct price ($38 founding / $48 retail)? Correct claims (15 seconds, matte, fragrance-free)? No unsubstantiated claims?
5. **Compliance:** No "clinically proven" without trial data? Cosmetic classification (not drug)? Claims use "helps", "supports", "designed to" language?
6. **Logo:** Orange period present and round? Wordmark at Montserrat 800 (heavy), not a lighter weight? Clear space maintained? No effects or distortions?
