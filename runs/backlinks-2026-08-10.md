## Unlock action: affiliate program

Nearly every Tier 1 site monetizes via affiliate links. Without a program, inclusion odds drop hard; with one, you're revenue for them. A simple Shopify affiliate app (or ShareASale) at 10–15% commission is the single highest-leverage move for this entire list. Do this before outreach, mention it in every pitch.
