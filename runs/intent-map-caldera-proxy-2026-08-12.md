# Intent Map (Competitor Proxy) — Caldera + Lab — 2026-08-12

**What this is**: Base Layer has zero GSC query data (0 impressions, 0/59 indexed as of Aug 12), so this map is built from a competitor's public footprint instead of our own rankings: Caldera + Lab's full sitemap (210 URLs crawled live) plus 6 live SERP probes. **No search volumes appear anywhere in this file** — no Ahrefs data exists for this run, and inventing volumes would be worse than omitting them. Every ranking claim below was verified in a live SERP today; everything else is labeled as inference.

**Why Caldera + Lab**: closest positioning mirror in the market — premium men's skincare, mountain-west brand story (Jackson Hole vs Breckenridge), DTC-first, and they sell a moisturizer literally named "The Base Layer." Established brand (Nordstrom retail, Amazon presence, celebrity endorsers), so their content architecture reflects real investment decisions.

## ⚠️ Brand collision (verified live, highest-priority finding)

Caldera's rich moisturizer is named **"The Base Layer"** — $65 one-time / $52 subscription at calderalab.com/products/the-base-layer (verified from PDP structured data, 2026-08-12), also sold via Amazon and Nordstrom.

Live SERP for **"base layer moisturizer for men"**: Nordstrom's Caldera listing #1, Amazon's Caldera listing #2, calderalab.com PDP #4, plus Arc'teryx/Bass Pro apparel base layers filling the rest. **Our own brand-adjacent query currently belongs to a competitor's product and to clothing.**

Consequences:
1. Every shopper who hears "Base Layer" and googles "base layer moisturizer" today lands on Caldera. Branded-search leakage from day one of any ad spend.
2. The defensive move is also the highest-intent content play available: a **Base Layer vs Caldera Lab comparison** — the vs-series already exists (Brickell, CeraVe, Cetaphil, Kiehl's, Neutrogena) and Caldera is arguably the most important missing entry. Someone searching the two names together is deep in purchase mode *and* disambiguating the name collision.
3. Integrity rule applies as always: ingredient-label analysis with the standard disclosure block, no claimed wear-testing. Price contrast is legitimately dramatic and verified: their Base Layer moisturizer alone is $65 ($52 on subscription) and The Regimen set is $220, vs our $38 single / $35 subscription.

## Cluster analysis — their architecture vs ours

### 1. Serum education (their biggest cluster: ~9 articles)
`a-guide-to-mens-face-serums`, `what-is-a-serum`, `serum-vs-toner`, `should-you-use-serum-before-or-after-moisturizers`, `what-does-serum-do-for-your-face`, `choosing-the-best-face-serum`, `anti-aging-face-serums`, `how-to-choose-a-natural-face-serum`, `try-our-antioxidant-serum`

- **SERP evidence**: "serum before or after moisturizer" → Caldera **ranks page 1 (~#6)** against MedicalNewsToday. "best face serum for men" → Caldera absent; Brickell's guide is #1.
- **Pattern that matters**: their *question-format* articles earn rankings; their *listicle/guide* formats don't. Question-format content is the transferable lesson.
- **Base Layer coverage**: zero serum content — and no serum product.
- **Verdict — counter-position, don't copy.** We shouldn't write serum guides for a product we don't sell. The honest angle is the one only we can write: **"Do men actually need a 5-step routine?"** — routine-minimalism content that answers the same searcher questions (do I need a serum, what order do products go in) from the all-in-one position. It feeds `/all-in-one-skincare-for-men` and directly counters Caldera's regimen upsell.

### 2. Moisturizer (4 articles)
`best-moisturizer-for-dry-skin`, `why-use-a-mens-moisturizer-regularly`, `what-to-look-for-in-an-organic-face-moisturizer`, product PDPs

- **SERP evidence**: "best moisturizer for dry skin men" → Caldera absent. SERP is retailers + derm authorities + notably weak small blogs (henkeys.com, blue-print.co, skinsort profile pages) in the middle positions.
- **Base Layer coverage**: 11 moisturizer articles including exact-match `best-moisturizer-dry-skin-men`. **We out-architect them here already.** The weak middle of that SERP says these positions are winnable once indexed.
- **Verdict — hold, no new content.** This validates the existing moisturizer hub; the blocker is indexing, not architecture.

### 3. Skincare mistakes (1 article — their best performer we found)
- **SERP evidence**: "skincare mistakes men make" → Caldera **#2**, behind only Schweiger Dermatology. Rest of SERP is tiny DTC blogs.
- **Base Layer coverage**: `/articles/skincare-mistakes` exists — direct head-to-head.
- **Verdict — optimize existing page.** Caldera at #2 proves a DTC brand blog can own this query. Worth a quality pass on our article (question-format H2s, extractable summary) once indexing lands.

### 4. Anti-aging / over-40 (5+ articles, mostly lifestyle)
`workout-plans-for-men-over-40`, `a-guide-to-losing-weight-over-40`, `how-to-prevent-and-delay-wrinkles`, `improve-your-skin-elasticity`, `healthy-lifestyle-choices-to-prevent-aging`

- **SERP evidence**: "men's skincare routine over 40 anti aging" → Caldera absent. SERP is entirely small DTC blogs (Anthony, Jaxon Lane, Strive, Homo Naturals) — no authority sites, no retailers.
- **Base Layer coverage**: `best-moisturizer-men-over-40` (just re-edited + republished) and `aging-wrinkles-men` concern page — but **no routine-over-40 article**.
- **Verdict — one new spoke.** "Men's skincare routine over 40" hub-spokes cleanly into the existing over-40 moisturizer article and the aging concern page. Weakest verified SERP of the six probes.

### 5. Cleanser (3 articles)
`how-to-choose-the-best-mens-facial-cleanser`, `whats-are-the-best-ingredients-in-a-natural-face-cleanser`, `bar-soap-your-face`

- **Base Layer coverage**: zero — and no cleanser product.
- **Verdict — one question-format spoke, product-agnostic.** "Is bar soap bad for your face?" is a real question men ask, answerable honestly, and funnels naturally into the 3-step routine hub. Low priority.

### 6. Lifestyle bloat (≈15 articles)
Yoga poses, organic food guides, blue light, adaptogenic herbs, essential oils, carbon footprint, athlete endorsement posts.

- **Verdict — ignore entirely.** This is classic 2021 DTC content-marketing bloat: off-topic, unranked (spot-checked), and it dilutes their topical authority. Do not replicate. Base Layer's tighter 59-URL, moisturizer-centric architecture is the better strategy — the KB seo-strategy article already codifies this.
